Our story

From our hearth to your table

Everything at Ember & Oak touches oak fire before it touches your plate — flatbreads blistered in 90 seconds, vegetables caramelized in cast iron, and dry-aged cuts rested over embers. Our menu changes with the market, but the fire never goes out.
